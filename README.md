Visualize your upcoming journey with clarity. See elevation changes, fuel-stop suggestions, and weighbridge positions before you even turn the key.

### ⚙️ Live Telemetry Panel
Watch your vitals in real time — speed, RPM, fuel consumption, cargo integrity, and fatigue levels — all in a beautifully rendered overlay that stays out of the way until you need it.

### 🧩 Modular Configuration Profiles
Save different setups for different jobs. A heavy-cargo profile, a night-driving profile, an economy-run profile. Swap between them instantly.

### 🔔 Smart Notification Center
No more missing a critical alert. The notification system prioritizes what matters and quietly files the rest for later review.

### 🛠️ Built-In Diagnostics
Something feels off? The diagnostic panel helps you understand what is happening under the hood without ever needing to leave the cab.

### 🎨 Themeable Skins
Prefer dark mode for those late-night hauls? Prefer a warm amber glow for a vintage feel? Choose from a curated collection of themes, or craft your own.

### 🧠 Adaptive Learning Suggestions
Over time, the companion learns your driving patterns and offers gentle suggestions — never commands — to help you improve fuel economy and reduce fatigue.

## 🛠️ Setup Guide (Detailed Walkthrough)

Setting up the companion is designed to be as smooth as a freshly paved motorway. Follow along at your own pace.

### Step 1 — Acquire the Package
Use the entry line above to obtain the latest build. Confirm the checksum matches the value published in the releases notes.

### Step 2 — Unpack in a Safe Location
Choose a folder you will remember. Avoid cluttering your desktop; a dedicated directory such as one named after the companion itself keeps things tidy.

### Step 3 — First Launch Ritual
On the very first run, the companion performs a brief initialization. This usually takes under a minute. Let it finish without interrupting; it is simply getting its bearings, much like warming up a cold engine on a winter morning.

### Step 4 — Configure Your Preferences
The setup wizard will greet you with a warm welcome. Choose your language, pick a theme, and select your preferred unit system (metric or imperial — we do not judge).

### Step 5 — Bind Your Hotkeys
Assign keys that feel natural to your fingers. The companion comes with sensible defaults, but personalization is where the experience truly shines.

### Step 6 — Pair With Your Session
Start your trucking session first, then bring the companion to the foreground. It will detect the session automatically in most cases. If it does not, a manual pairing option is available in the settings panel.

### Step 7 — Take a Short Test Drive
Before a long haul, take a five-minute test run. Confirm that telemetry readings update smoothly and that notifications appear as expected.

### Step 8 — You Are Ready
That is it. The open road awaits, and your new co-pilot is riding shotgun.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this affiliated with the original game studio?**
No. This is an independent, community-driven project built by enthusiasts.

**Does it modify any game files?**
The companion operates externally and does not alter the base game installation.

**Will it work on a laptop?**
Absolutely, provided your laptop meets the recommended specifications listed above.

**Can I use it with peripheral hardware like wheel controllers?**
Yes. The companion coexists peacefully with wheel, pedal, and shifter setups.

**Is the companion available for other platforms?**
Currently the focus is on Windows desktop environments. Broader platform discussions happen regularly in the community.

**How often is it updated?**

**What if I encounter a problem?**
